How it holds

Meet the mechanism.

You can't prove a payment didn't happen. So PayGo flips it: default is the resting state, and a proof of an on-time payment is the only thing that pushes it back.

Escrowed on Creditcoin

The seller locks the asset in the contract. Nobody — not even us — holds a lockout button. It moves only on proof, or on default.

contract · not custody

Paid on Ethereum

Each installment is paid in USDC where your money already lives — one-click permit, or sign once and autopay submits the rest.

permissionless

I don't pay
